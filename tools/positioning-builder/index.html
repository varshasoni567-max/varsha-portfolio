Positioning Builder

Turn a few plain-language answers into a ready-to-use positioning statement.

  1. Positioning is the short answer to "who's this for, and why pick it over what they use today."
  2. Answer a few plain-language questions about your product, customer, and competitor.
  3. Get a finished statement — worded the way your chosen framework builds it. Switch frameworks and the argument itself changes, not just the wording.
