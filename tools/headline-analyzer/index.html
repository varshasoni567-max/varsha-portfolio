Headline Analyzer

Score your headline for power, emotion, length, and readability before you hit publish.

This tool scores a single headline — or several variants side by side — the way an editor would: word balance, emotional pull, length, and readability, rolled into a score out of 100. Use it on blog titles, email subject lines, ad copy, landing page headers, or social captions. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
